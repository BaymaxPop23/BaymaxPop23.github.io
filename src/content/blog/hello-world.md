## Welcome

Hello, I'm Sai -- a security researcher passionate about understanding how systems break and how to make them more resilient. This blog is where I'll be sharing my thoughts, findings, and experiences in the world of cybersecurity.

## What to Expect

Here's what you can look forward to reading about:

- **Vulnerability Research**: Deep dives into interesting vulnerabilities I discover or analyze
- **CTF Writeups**: Walkthroughs of capture-the-flag challenges
- **Security Tools**: Reviews and tutorials on tools that make our work more effective
- **Industry Thoughts**: Commentary on trends and developments in cybersecurity

## Why Blog?

I believe in the power of knowledge sharing. The security community thrives when researchers openly discuss their findings, methodologies, and lessons learned. Writing also helps me solidify my own understanding of complex topics.

## Getting Started

If you're new to security research, here are some resources I recommend:

1. Practice on platforms like HackTheBox and TryHackMe
2. Read CVE advisories and try to understand the root causes
3. Participate in CTF competitions
4. Join security communities and attend conferences

Stay tuned for more posts. Let's explore the fascinating world of security together.
